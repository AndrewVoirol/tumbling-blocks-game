## 1. Subsystem 1: Rigid-Body Tribology, Gravity, Wobbles & Tilts

### Issue 1.1 — Overdamped Flat-Face Slap Quenching Housner Rocking & Emitting Substep Impact Spam
- **Symptom & Root Cause**: In [`mujocoCalibratedParams.ts`](../src/physics/mujocoCalibratedParams.ts), primary block contact uses critical/overdamped Kelvin-Voigt parameters `contactSolref = [0.0042, 1.12]` ($\zeta = 1.12 > 1$) to hold 18-to-24-layer static stack sag below $0.20\text{ mm}$. Adversarial tilt probes releasing a free block across its $2.5\text{ cm}$ width onto its $1.25\text{ mm}$ chamfer at $4^\circ, 8^\circ$, and $12^\circ$ measured `signFlips = 1` and `sleepFrame = 3..4` (`50–67 ms`), quenching natural Housner ($e_{\text{rock}} = 1 - \frac{3}{2}\sin^2\alpha$) opposite-bevel rocking on the first flat slap while firing `4–5` duplicate `playGlassImpact` calls across consecutive substeps.
- **Mathematical & Algorithmic Fix**: In [`MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts`](../src/physics/MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts) (`lines 2681–2799`, `2906–2925`), unloaded horizontal prisms (`cachedClampingLoadN < 0.38 N`, `absUpY > 0.9984`, outside `PULLING`/`COLLAPSED`) transfer dissipative angular velocity onto the opposite $1.25\text{ mm}$ bevel pivot across up to $k < 5$ crossings ($e_0 = 0.74$ on oak, $0.76$ on glass), emitting rate-limited `WobbleRockEvent` records via `consumeWobbleEvents()` while suppressing generic `playGlassImpact` calls during active rocking sequences and preserving `sleepTolerance = 0.003`:

$$
\omega_{k+1}^+ = \min\!\left(9.5,\; \omega_k^- \cdot e_0 \cdot 0.88^k\right), \qquad v_y^+ = \min\!\left(0.20,\; 1.12\,\omega_{k+1}^+ b_{\text{half}} + 0.004\right)
$$
- **Measured Verification**: Free $4^\circ, 8^\circ, 12^\circ$ bevel tilts now exhibit **`3` angular-velocity sign reversals**, emit **`2–4` discrete `WobbleRockEvent`s** (`0` `playGlassImpact` substep duplicates, `0` false damage), and reach `100%` sleep in `6–9` frames (`100–150 ms`). `marginalOverhangTopple` (`+3.9 cm` / `5.8°`) topples dynamically in `15` frames (`< 45` gate), while `18L` sag (`0.0980 mm`) and `24L` sag (`0.1991 mm`) remain unchanged.

### Issue 1.2 — Multi-Block Collapse Burst Spiking `stepMsP95` to `28.62 ms`

## 2. Subsystem 2: Brittle Fracture & Progressive Crazing

Empirical drop-height sweeps onto `oak_table` measured `13.0 cm` minimum shatter height at `damage = 0.0` and `13.0 cm` at `damage = 0.85` (`0.00%` weakening). Three-point flexural snap (`42.0 MPa`) likewise ignored `blk.damage`.
- **Mathematical & Algorithmic Fix**: Replaced the inverted clamp in [`MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts`](../src/physics/MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts) (`lines 2845–2905`) with Griffith sub-critical flaw toughness degradation ($d = \operatorname{clamp}(d_{\text{eff}}, 0, 0.95)$), where three-point Euler-Bernoulli bending stress $\sigma_b = \frac{6 M_{\text{bend}}}{b h^2}$ ($M_{\text{bend}} = \frac{1}{4} F_{\text{bend}} L_{\text{span}}$) is gated safely above the $\approx 12.5\text{ N}$ static weight of an 18-course tower via $F_{\text{top}} > 38(1 - 0.38\,d)\text{ N}$ and $F_{\text{sup}} > 24(1 - 0.38\,d)\text{ N}$:

$$
v_{\text{shatter}}(d) = v_{\text{shatter},0}\,(1 - 0.36\,d), \qquad \sigma_{\text{flex,crit}}(d) = 0.75\,(1 - 0.48\,d)\text{ MPa}
$$
- **Measured Verification** ([`tests/physicsColosseum.test.ts`](../tests/physicsColosseum.test.ts) `C1`): Minimum oak-table shatter drop height decreases from **`13.5 cm`** ($d = 0.0$) to **`8.5 cm`** ($d = 0.55$, **`-37.0%`**) and **`6.5 cm`** ($d = 0.85$, **`-51.9%`**). ## 8. Post-Colosseum Stateful Sandbox, Polariscope Translucency & Telemetry Edge-Case Remediation

During interactive Sandbox play (`Free Physics` + `Vespers` illumination) on commit `0b3d585`, three runtime defects were identified across multi-stage fracture/collapse sequences:

1. **Mid-Air Frozen Voronoi Shards After Secondary Collapse or Bounce Apex (`MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts`)**:
   - **Root Cause**: `dampGroundedMicroJitterShardsSubstep()` previously evaluated `longLivedLowEnergy = yM < 0.22 && s.quietSubsteps > 280 && vSi < 0.32 && Math.abs(vy) < 0.16` using only coordinate elevation ($y < 22\text{ cm}$, spanning 81% of the $27\text{ cm}$ tower height) and monotonic spawn age (`s.quietSubsteps`), **without verifying upward contact support**. Whenever a shard older than $0.96\text{ s}$ (`s.quietSubsteps > 580`) either crossed the vertical velocity apex ($v_y \approx 0$) of a bounce arc below $22\text{ cm}$ or lost the intact block supporting it, a single `mj_step` accelerated it downward by only $|v_y| = g\,\Delta t \approx 0.027\text{ m/s} < 0.16\text{ m/s}$, which `zeroSnap` immediately overwrote to `qvel = 0` on every substep—putting the shard to sleep (`tree_asleep >= 0`) suspended in mid-air. Secondarily, `shatterBlock()` and `wakeActiveTowerBlocks()` only woke `blockBodyIds`, leaving sleeping elevated `shardBodyIds` asleep when supporting blocks shattered or buckled, and `triggerSandboxCollapseImpulse()` applied fixed 18-course height thresholds that under-torqued already-shortened ($< 16$-course) Sandbox stacks.
   - **Fix**: Above the static oak tabletop ($y \ge 2.6\text{ cm}$), micro-jitter damping in [`MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts`](../src/physics/MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts) (`lines 4065–4125`) now tracks consecutive zero-support substeps via `d.qfrc_constraint[dofAdr + 1] > bodyMass * 1.5` (`shardNoSupportSubsteps[slot] <= 4`, tolerating $\le 6.7\text{ ms}$ of facet chatter on shard piles while guaranteeing zero damping on airborne arcs or unsupported shards), and relieves grounded hero shards ($y < 2.4\text{ cm}$, age $> 0.7\text{ s}$) pinched above $2.5\text{ N}$ ($> 25\times$ shard weight) beneath heavy collapsed blocks so `zeroSnap` never turns a thin splinter into a buzzing solver wedge. `shatterBlock()` (`lines 3194–3239`), [`mujocoWorldCompat.ts`](../src/physics/mujocoWorldCompat.ts) (`wakeActiveTowerBlocks` / `wakeAllActiveShards`, `lines 188–255`), and an acyclic unsupported-sleep sweep on elevated hero shards ($y > 4.5\text{ cm}$) in `syncTransformsAndRules()` (`lines 4305–4346`) wake any sleeping elevated shard whose supporting block or lower hero shard is removed, while `triggerSandboxCollapseImpulse()` (`lines 1732–1765`) normalizes shear layers when `maxIntactLayer < 16`.

2. **Opaque Matte-Black Unstressed Cutouts in Polariscope Mode (`glassShard.wgsl.ts` & `glassTower.wgsl.ts`)**:
   - **Root Cause**: Cross-polarized dark-field branches in [`glassShard.wgsl.ts`](../src/renderer/shaders/glassShard.wgsl.ts) (`lines 378–395`) and [`glassTower.wgsl.ts`](../src/renderer/shaders/glassTower.wgsl.ts) (`lines 940–956`) previously scaled Beer-Lambert-absorbed background transmission by `0.042` (shards) and `0.14` (blocks) while suppressing Fresnel reflection on shards by `94%` (`reflTerm * 0.06`).    - **Fix**: Both shaders now reconstruct passband/luma-neutralized smoked-crystal background refraction (`~30%` clear neutral-density transmission on unloaded glass, deepening to `0.11` inside stressed tower courses via `mix(0.30, 0.11, stressGate)` so spectral $\sin^2(\pi\Delta/\lambda)$ fringes retain dark-field trough contrast), preserve `50–72%` $s$-polarized Brewster oblique bevel/cleavage speculars (`dBrewster * dBrewster`, avoiding negative-base `pow()` `NaN`s in WGSL), and retain cleavage-edge retardation glints on shards.

3. **Outlier-Skewed `Plumb` (`21.8°` / `45.0°`) & Collapsed Sandbox Heap `Elevation` (`MujocoPhysicsEngine.ts` & `App.tsx`)**:
